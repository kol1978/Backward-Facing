Detailed Description

template<class Type, class GeoMesh>
class Foam::SlicedDimensionedField< Type, GeoMesh >

Specialisation of DimensionedField which holds a slice of a given complete field in such a form that it acts as a DimensionedField.

The destructor is wrapped to avoid deallocation of the storage of the complete fields when this is destroyed.
