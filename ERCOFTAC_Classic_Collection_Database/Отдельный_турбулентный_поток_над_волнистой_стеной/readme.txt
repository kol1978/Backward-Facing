The files xlam00.dat to xlam09.dat contain data of case wb3 from 
Hudson (1993) at 10 streamwise positions, where

y: 	height over the surface in [cm]
U:	mean velocity in the x direction, in [cm/s]
V:	mean velocity in the y direction, in [cm/s]
u':	rms fluctuating velocity in the x direction, in [cm/s]
v':	rms fluctuating velocity in the y direction, in [cm/s]
u'v':   Reynolds shear stress, in [(cm/s)^2]

For other quantities refer to the data files.

U_b= 12 cm/s 	        : bulk velocity in the lower channel half
Re_h=U_b*h / nu= 3380	: Reynolds number

The friction velocity at the wavy wall is u_tau_wa = 1.29 cm/s.

The file pressure.dat contains pressure data, calculated from the 
velocity data by Hudson.
