class  lumpedMassTemperatureFvPatchScalarField
 This boundary condition is applied to a patch which bounds a solid body, wholly or partially. It represents the body as a lumped mass, i.e. by a single temperature T which is fixed across the patch. The body has a volume V which is either specified by the user, or is calculated when the patch describes a closed volume (including in 2D meshes).
