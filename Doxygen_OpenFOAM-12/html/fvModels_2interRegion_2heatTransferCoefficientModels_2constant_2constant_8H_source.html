27Description
28 Constant heat transfer model. The heat transfer coefficient [W/m^2/K] (htc)
29 must be provided as a value in the coefficients dictionary or as a field in
30 constant.
31
32Usage
33 Example usage:
34 \verbatim
35 {
36 heatTransferCoefficientModel constant;
37
38 htc 1e5;
39 }
40 \endverbatim
