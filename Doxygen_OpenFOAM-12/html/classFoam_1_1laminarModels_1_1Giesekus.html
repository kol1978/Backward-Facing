Detailed Description

template<class BasicMomentumTransportModel>
class Foam::laminarModels::Giesekus< BasicMomentumTransportModel >

Giesekus model for viscoelasticity using the upper-convected time derivative of the stress tensor with support for multiple modes.

Reference: 

    Giesekus, H., 1982.
    A simple constitutive equation for polymer fluids based on the
    concept of deformation-dependent tensional mobility.
    J. Non-Newton. Fluid. 11, 69–109.
