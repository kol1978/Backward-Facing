Detailed Description

Mesh motion solver simple linear expansion and contraction of a mesh region defined by a motion axis and the extents of the motion.

Example: 

    mover           motionSolver;

    libs            ("libfvMotionSolvers.so");

    motionSolver    displacementLinearMotion;

    axis            (0 1 0);
    xFixed          0.8;
    xMoving         0;
    displacement    table
    (
        (0 0)
        (4 0.7)
    );

This mesh is compressed between xFixed and xMoving in the direction axis between time 0 and 4 with a maximum displacement of 0.7. The mesh beyond xFixed is fixed and beyond xMoving moves with maximum displacement.
