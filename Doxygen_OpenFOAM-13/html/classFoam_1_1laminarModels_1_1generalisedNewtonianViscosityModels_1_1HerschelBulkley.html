Detailed Description

Herschel-Bulkley generalised Newtonian viscosity model.

The Herschel-Bulkley model combines the effects of a Bingham plastic and power-law behaviour of a fluid. At a low strain rate the fluid adopts a viscosity . Beyond a threshold stress , the viscosity is described as a power law.

Kinematic viscosity [m^2/s]

Example specification: 

     viscosityModel HerschelBulkley;

     tau0       0.01;
     k          0.001;
     n          0.2;

Note the viscosity nu0 at zero strain rate is a physical property, generally specified in the physicalProperties file.
