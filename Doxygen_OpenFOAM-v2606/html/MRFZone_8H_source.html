28Class
29 Foam::MRFZone
30
31Description
32 MRF zone definition based on cell zone and parameters
33 obtained from a control dictionary constructed from the given stream.
34
35 The rotation of the MRF region is defined by an origin and axis of
36 rotation and an angular speed.
