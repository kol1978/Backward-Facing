28Class
29 Foam::LBFGS
30
31Description
32 The quasi-Newton Limited-memory BFGS formula. Keeps nPrevSteps_ of the
33 y and s vectors and approximates the inverse areas through them.
34 Values of 3 < nPrevSteps_ < 20 are suggested.
