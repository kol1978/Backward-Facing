Detailed Description

Core routines used when reading/writing ABAQUS files.

Face mappings for abaqus deduced from libmesh internals

Tet4 cells

Face OpenFOAM libmesh abaqus starcd
(1 2 3) 0 2 2 5
(0 3 2) 1 3 3 4
(0 1 3) 2 1 1 2
(0 2 1) 3 0 0 0

Pyr5 cells

Face OpenFOAM libmesh abaqus starcd
(0 3 2 1) 0 4 n/a 0
(0 4 3) 1 3 n/a 4
(3 4 2) 2 2 n/a 3
(1 2 4) 3 1 n/a 5
(0 1 4) 4 0 n/a 2

Prism6 cells

Face OpenFOAM libmesh abaqus starcd
(0 2 1) 0 0 0 0
(3 4 5) 1 4 1 1
(0 3 5 2) 2 3 4 4
(1 2 5 4) 3 2 3 5
(0 1 4 3) 4 1 2 2

Hex8 cells

Face OpenFOAM libmesh abaqus starcd
(0 4 7 3) 0 4 5 4
(1 2 6 5) 1 2 3 5
(0 1 5 4) 2 1 2 2
(3 7 6 2) 3 3 4 3
(0 3 2 1) 4 0 0 0
(4 5 6 7) 5 5 1 1
