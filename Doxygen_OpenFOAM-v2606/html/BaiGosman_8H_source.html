26Class
27 Foam::localInteractionModels::BaiGosman
28
29Description
30 Internal BaiGosman thermo parcel-patch interaction used by
31 Foam::LocalInteraction.
32
33 Requires a parcel type exposing T() and rho() (thermo / reacting
34 clouds) and an SLGThermo with at least one liquid component
35 registered on the cloud's database. Splash parcels are added to the
36 owner cloud immediately via Cloud::addParticle.
37
