28 Foam::VectorSpace

30Description
31 Templated vector space.
32
33 Template arguments are the Form the vector space will be used to create,
34 the type of the elements and the number of elements.
35
36 The VectorSpace will normally contain trivial component types,
37 but since there are certain places that use/misuse Vector to store
38 a tuple of other things, we cannot categorically assert that the
39 components are "trivially copyable".
