Detailed Description

Binary diffusion coefficient polynomial function of the log of temperature correcting the standard kinetic theory temperature dependence. 

    Dji = T^(3/2)*(c0 + c1*lnT + c2*lnT^2 + c3*lnT^3 + c4*lnT^4)/p
