Detailed Description

This source condition creates an interfacial growth transfer source term in the group surface-area-to-volume-ratio (kappa) equations of the fractal shape model of a population balance model, consistent with interfacial mass transfer of phase change or similar.

Usage
Example of the source condition specification: 
<sourceName>
{
    type            interfacialGrowthGroupSurfaceAreaVolumeRatio;
    libs            ("libmultiphaseEulerFvModels.so");
